Collaboration

How I work with people

I’m easy to work with. I ask questions, I listen, and I keep people updated.

01

Clear Communication

If I have a question, I ask. If I finish something, I tell you. I don’t like it when people have to chase me for an update.

02

Team Collaboration

I like bouncing ideas with the team. When someone gives feedback, I actually use it, even if it means changing a part I already liked.

03

Creative Problem Solving

If a page or flow feels confusing, I try another layout. I keep going until it’s easier to use, not just nicer to look at.

04

Time Management

I plan my work around the deadline. I don’t wait until the last day, and I leave time to fix the small mistakes.

Value

Why work with me?

I can design the site and build it, so the process is faster and there’s less back and forth.

Clean Code

I try to keep my code organized so it’s easy to edit later. Messy files just make the next change harder.

Eye for Design

I pay attention to spacing, fonts, and colors. Those small things are what make a site feel put together.

Responsive Design

I check how the site looks on a phone, not only on a laptop. Most people will open it on mobile anyway.

Fast Delivery

I work fast, but I still review everything before I send it. I’d rather turn in something clean than something rushed.
